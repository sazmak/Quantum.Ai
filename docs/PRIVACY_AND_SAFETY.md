# Privacy and safety

Baseline principles for School AI / Quantum AI pre-pilot work.
This is not a finished legal policy. A school-specific data policy must be agreed before an expanded student pilot.

## Principles

1. **Minimal personal data** — collect only what the pilot needs.
2. **No unnecessary student identifiers** — avoid names, emails, student IDs in early eval sets when anonymized questions suffice.
3. **Approved educational sources only** — do not scrape arbitrary web content as the school knowledge base.
4. **Teacher / school approval** — materials and broader access require school-side approval.
5. **Transparent sources** — show what the answer relied on when possible.
6. **Ability to say "I don't know"** — refuse or defer when school sources are insufficient.
7. **AI output is not automatically correct** — humans (teachers, students) remain responsible for academic use.
8. **Human oversight** — teacher review before expanding access.

## Pre-pilot practices

- Prefer teacher-provided, anonymized questions for evaluation
- Keep demo / closed prototype separate from production student data
- Do not claim production-grade compliance until reviewed with the school
- Expanded pilot only after school agreement and a written data policy

## Academic integrity

- Design toward help and understanding, not unchecked answer dumping
- Tutor Mode and practice-first workflows are planned mitigations (not yet MVP)
- Final integrity rules belong to the school

## Incidents

Any suspected privacy leak or unsafe behavior: pause student access, notify the school contact, document, fix before resume.

## Non-claims

This document does not assert GDPR/local-law certification, a signed DPA, or parental consent already obtained.
