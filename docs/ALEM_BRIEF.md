# ALEM brief — School AI / Quantum AI

Short briefing for **ALEM AI Talent Lab**.

School AI is a school-specific educational AI platform. The first deployment target is **Quantum AI** for **Quantum STEM School**. We are in **technical MVP → pre-pilot**: honest about what exists, clear about what must be validated.

## What exists

- Closed web prototype (not production): https://quantum-ai.grok.me/
- Chat UI, retrieval over **demo notes**, openable sources, usefulness rating, session export
- Product docs, pilot plan, privacy principles, and evaluation approach in this repository
- Technical approach: **RAG-first** — retrieve approved school material, then answer with sources

**Not yet:** connected LLM on real Quantum STEM materials, real student pilot, teacher-validated quality metrics, production deployment.

## Why Quantum STEM

- One school keeps the corpus small, auditable, and curriculum-aligned
- Informatics is a concrete first subject for a narrow pilot
- Teachers can approve materials and judge answer quality
- Success or failure is measurable before any scale story

## What we want to validate

1. Do school-grounded answers beat generic chatbots for homework / understanding on approved topics?
2. Are citations and refusal-when-insufficient workable in practice?
3. Will a teacher endorse a limited student trial after reviewing eval samples?
4. Is exam prep a recurring use case worth building after core Q&A quality?

Hypotheses only until measured. No PMF or learning-gain claims.

## Why ALEM can help

ALEM is a strong fit as a **technology and mentorship partner**, not only as a funding source:

- Rigorous review of RAG / eval methodology
- Guidance on safe school deployment
- Access to model and compute options sized to a pilot
- Research discipline: measure before scale

## Potential support

- AI/ML mentorship and technical review
- Model / API access or compute guidance for v0.2
- Privacy-safe deployment review for school context
- Support structuring the Quantum STEM pilot and evals
- Research support on educational AI evaluation
- Infrastructure advice tied to pilot requirements (not hardware for its own sake)

## Ask

Help us turn a working retrieval prototype into a **measured** school pilot — with clear stop/continue criteria — starting with one Quantum STEM informatics topic.
